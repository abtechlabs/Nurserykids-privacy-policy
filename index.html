Privacy Policy

Nursery Kids respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our app operates and handles data.

Information Collection

Our app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. It functions entirely offline and does not require internet access or any permissions.

Data Usage

All learning content (letters, numbers, colors, shapes, animals, and games) is stored locally within the app. No user data is transmitted or recorded.

Third‑Party Content

Nursery Kids does not display ads or link to external websites. All images and sounds are used solely for educational purposes.

Security

Since the app does not collect user data, there is no risk of personal information exposure. The app is designed for safe offline use by children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
